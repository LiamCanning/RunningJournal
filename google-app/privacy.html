Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

Liam Sync is a private tool used only by its owner. It reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheets and Drive files to keep personal dashboards up to date.

No data from any other person is collected. Google data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
